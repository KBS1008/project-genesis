# ART DIRECTION

**Project:** Project Genesis

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Planning

---

# Vision

Project Genesis is a modern industrial and economic simulation.

The visual identity shall communicate:

- intelligence
- precision
- industrial progress
- strategic decision making
- technological evolution
- corporate growth

The player should feel like:

> the CEO of a global industrial corporation,
> not the mayor of a city,
> not a soldier,
> not a fantasy hero.

Every visual decision shall reinforce this identity.

---

# Core Identity

Project Genesis is:

Industrial.

Economic.

Strategic.

Professional.

Global.

Analytical.

Elegant.

Modern.

Purposeful.

Project Genesis is NOT:

Fantasy

Steampunk

Sci-Fi Space Opera

Cartoon

Comic

Mobile Game

Cyberpunk

Neon UI

Retro Pixel Art

Military Shooter

Casual Simulation

---

# Emotional Goals

The interface should create:

confidence

clarity

control

strategic thinking

long-term planning

industrial achievement

The player should never feel overwhelmed by decoration.

The interface should always increase understanding.

---

# Design Statement

Project Genesis should look like the control center of a multinational corporation operating industrial infrastructure across the world.

The experience should resemble:

• an executive command center

• a logistics headquarters

• an engineering planning office

• a financial operations center

Every screen should reinforce the feeling that the player is managing an industrial empire.

---

# Visual Tone

Professional.

Calm.

Confident.

Sophisticated.

Technical.

Structured.

Readable.

Elegant.

Minimal.

---

# Artistic Inspirations

The project takes inspiration from the visual language—not the artwork—of:

Capitalism Lab

Workers & Resources

Factorio

Dyson Sphere Program

Anno 1800 (management interfaces)

Power BI

Grafana

Bloomberg Terminal

Modern ERP Systems

Industrial SCADA Software

Business Intelligence Dashboards

No single title shall dominate the visual identity.

Project Genesis shall establish its own recognizable style.

---

# Industrial Aesthetic

Architecture should emphasize:

steel

glass

concrete

machinery

engineering

automation

clean industry

renewable energy

high technology

Factories should feel functional rather than decorative.

---

# Corporate Identity

The player controls a company.

Therefore:

corporate branding

financial information

management reports

executive dashboards

performance indicators

must be visually central.

The interface shall resemble professional management software.

---

# Technology

Technology should appear:

advanced

credible

engineered

modular

scientific

Avoid magical visual effects.

Avoid unrealistic holographic interfaces.

---

# World

The world should feel:

alive

productive

economically connected

industrialized

regional

dynamic

not empty.

Every region should communicate:

identity

resources

specialization

infrastructure

economic role

---

# Buildings

Buildings should prioritize:

recognizability

production purpose

industrial realism

functional silhouettes

Large industrial complexes should feel impressive through scale rather than ornament.

---

# Transportation

Transport is the bloodstream of the economy.

Vehicles should emphasize:

capacity

reliability

efficiency

industrial engineering

Roads

Rail

Ports

Air Cargo

Pipelines (future)

should appear as infrastructure—not decoration.

---

# Resources

Resources should be instantly recognizable.

Icons should emphasize:

material

shape

purpose

Industrial resources should feel tangible.

---

# Research

Research represents technological evolution.

Visual language should resemble:

engineering drawings

technical documentation

laboratories

blueprints

innovation

Avoid fantasy spell trees.

---

# Economy

Economic information is one of the game's defining characteristics.

Prices

Supply

Demand

Liquidity

Cash Flow

Profit

Expenses

should always be easy to read.

Charts are primary gameplay elements.

---

# Data Visualization

Charts are not decorative.

Charts ARE gameplay.

They should support:

decision making

trend recognition

problem detection

planning

The player should never struggle to interpret a chart.

---

# Maps

Maps are strategic tools.

Not illustrations.

Layers should communicate:

ownership

resources

transport

economy

infrastructure

trade

selection

Maps must prioritize readability.

---

# Characters

Project Genesis is not character-driven.

Employees exist.

Managers exist.

Executives exist.

Portraits should support management.

They should never dominate the interface.

Characters are secondary to the company.

---

# User Interface

The UI should disappear behind the information.

Players should notice:

their company

their economy

their strategy

—not the interface itself.

---

# Motion

Animation should communicate:

change

completion

selection

transition

progress

Animation shall never distract from gameplay.

---

# Sound Relationship

Visuals and sound should reinforce each other.

Industrial ambience.

Machines.

Transport.

Construction.

Research.

Financial notifications.

Everything should feel part of one coherent system.

---

# Color Psychology

Colors communicate meaning.

Never decoration.

Green

Growth

Profit

Operational

Blue

Information

Technology

Research

Yellow

Attention

Planning

Orange

Warning

Red

Critical

Loss

Failure

Gray

Neutral

Background

Purple

Innovation

Advanced Research

The palette should remain restrained and professional.

---

# Typography

Typography communicates hierarchy.

Not personality.

Readable.

Professional.

Consistent.

Large numbers must remain readable at a glance.

Financial information deserves particular emphasis.

---

# Lighting

Soft.

Industrial.

Neutral.

Avoid dramatic cinematic lighting.

Factories should appear productive.

Not mysterious.

---

# Illustration Style

Semi-realistic.

Modern.

Industrial.

Architectural.

Detailed enough to communicate function.

Simple enough to remain readable.

---

# Iconography

Icons should appear:

engineered

precise

minimal

consistent

technical

Avoid cartoon proportions.

---

# Dashboard Philosophy

Every dashboard should answer:

What is happening?

Why?

What requires attention?

What should the player do next?

If a dashboard cannot answer these questions, it is incomplete.

---

# Information Hierarchy

Critical Alerts

↓

Financial Health

↓

Production

↓

Research

↓

Transport

↓

Markets

↓

Regional Details

↓

Historical Analytics

The player's attention should naturally follow this order.

---

# Long-Term Evolution

As the company grows:

the interface should feel more powerful,

not more complicated.

Complexity should emerge from gameplay,

never from visual clutter.

---

# Artistic Constraints

Avoid:

gratuitous animation

overly glossy effects

glassmorphism

neon gradients

visual noise

skeuomorphism

oversized shadows

overly rounded interfaces

fantasy ornamentation

The interface should age well over many years.

---

# Design Goals

A player should immediately recognize a screenshot of Project Genesis.

Without seeing the logo.

Without reading the title.

The visual identity should become distinctive through:

clarity

industrial aesthetics

information density

professional dashboards

high-quality charts

consistent visual language

—not through excessive decoration.
